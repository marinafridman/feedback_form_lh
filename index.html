Thank you for helping us test LocalHarvest!

LocalHarvest is intended to be a secure tool that turns tables in sensitive PDFs into structured data you can search and analyze. Your documents never leave your machine. The app is powered by a local AI model that runs on your computer.

Currently the tool is optimized for parsing tables in corporate ownership documents, such as registers of shareholders and subsidiary lists. It can be expanded to other types of tables, and we encourage you to explore this once testing with the sample documents is complete.

The tool offers two functions: finding interesting pages in long documents (“triage”) and reading those pages to pull out the data you are looking for (“extract”).

Our goal for this round of testing is to get feedback about the functionality and experience of using the app. Testing takes approximately 2 hours, not counting the time the app spends processing. If you would like to record your screen while you use the app, that would be helpful for the developers, but it’s not required.

Instructions for use

Tick off each step as you go. Your progress is remembered in this browser.

  1. Restart your computer and close all other applications. While the app is running, please don’t use any other windows or programs, so that the processing times reflect the app alone.
  2. Open the installation file you received. Open the app. Follow the instructions in the app to download the libraries the app needs and the recommended model.
  3. Several example documents have been provided. Load them into the app one by one and select the option “Find the pages with tables”:
    • Mandeville
    • Rema1000 annual report
  4. Not all computers will be able to run a model, and that is fine! If the app says “no models fit your computer”, please still look around: load the documents and explore the settings, because we’d love your feedback on that part too. Then skip to step 8 and the questionnaire.
  5. Click “Run” to run the extractor. Remember not to use other windows or programs while it’s running.
  6. For each document, record:
    • the processing time estimated by the app
    • the actual processing time
    You can type these straight into the questionnaire’s “Test results” section.
  7. Save the resulting files. You’ll upload them in the questionnaire.
  8. Copy the description of your computer (how fast it is and how much memory it has): in the app, go to the Settings tab in the left sidebar and scroll down to “This computer”. You’ll paste it into the questionnaire.
  9. Optional: test other documents. Corporate ownership tables can be run directly with the current prompt. For other types of tables, you can adjust the fields you want to extract in Settings » Edit instructions to the model. We’d love to hear about your experience, but you don’t need to share the actual documents or results with us if they are sensitive.
  10. Fill out the questionnaire.
